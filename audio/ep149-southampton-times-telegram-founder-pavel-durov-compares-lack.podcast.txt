The Titanic had lifeboats for roughly half its passengers. Everyone assumed the ship was unsinkable, so the boats were treated as decoration. Western democracies are making the same assumption about personal freedom right now, and the iceberg is already gouging the hull.

In the United Kingdom and Germany, people are facing fines and actual arrest for social media posts that insult politicians or violate nebulous standards of political correctness. The immediate result is entirely predictable. People are hiding their real identities online to survive.

Here is the catch. Governments in the United Kingdom and the European Union are pushing mandatory identity verification for social media access under the banner of child protection. But their own internal documents admit the actual goal is controlling platforms and monitoring political discourse. When officials say safety, they mean surveillance.

Banning platforms does not work. Teenagers bypass blocks using virtual private networks within minutes, which inadvertently dumps them into completely unfiltered spaces. But the bureaucrats do not care about the collateral damage. The European Union is pushing chat control legislation that would break digital encryption entirely. This would give the state the technical ability to read every single private message you send.

Consider what happens when governments hoard this data. It leaks. Corrupt officials sell it to criminal networks. Look at the recent string of kidnappings and extortion cases in France. That is your tax funding a surveillance apparatus that eventually arms the very criminals it claims to protect you from.

Authoritarian regimes intentionally write contradictory and impossible regulations. Compliance becomes a mathematical impossibility. This gives regulators the legal pretext to selectively target, fine, or imprison business owners who refuse secret, politically motivated demands to censor political opposition.

The Soviet secret police relied on human informants. Modern AI removes that limitation entirely. It gives any government the limitless ability to read, analyze, and map every private message, personal relationship, and passing thought you have.

There is no second West. If these democracies fully adopt authoritarian digital controls, there is nowhere left to escape. You build sovereignty through informed non-compliance with systems that do not serve you, or you watch the ship go down.
